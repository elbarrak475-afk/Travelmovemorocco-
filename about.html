Hello, we're TravelMoveMorocco

Two travelers, one big love for Morocco, and a mission to help you see it the way locals do.

Our story

TravelMoveMorocco began with a single trip: a wrong turn in the Fes medina, a glass of mint tea offered by a stranger, and the realization that Morocco is best experienced slowly, with curiosity and a little local guidance.

Since then we've wandered the souks of Marrakech, watched the sun set over the Merzouga dunes, and eaten far too much seafood in Essaouira. Every guide we write comes from those real journeys, not from a desk.

We started this blog so travelers could skip the tourist traps and spend their time (and money) on what makes Morocco unforgettable.

“Morocco is best experienced slowly.”
