How we review

We don't rewrite brochures. For every riad, desert camp or tour in this list, we look at the same things: how easy it is to find and reach, cleanliness and comfort, food, host and guide quality, and whether the price matches what you actually get.

Each review lists real pros and cons, because no place is perfect. If something isn't worth your money, we say so.

Our rating scale

  • 4.8–5.0: Exceptional, book with confidence
  • 4.4–4.7: Excellent, small trade-offs
  • 4.0–4.3: Good, right for the right traveler
  • Below 4.0: We don't recommend it
