Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Last updated: October 14, 2026

TravelMoveMorocco.com ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have when you visit our website.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address and message when you use our contact form or subscribe to our newsletter.
  • Automatically collected data: device type, browser, pages visited, approximate location and referring website, collected through cookies and similar technologies.

2. How we use your information

  • To reply to your messages and provide support.
  • To send newsletters, travel tips and guides you asked for.
  • To understand how visitors use the site and improve our content.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent abuse.

3. Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use them to remember preferences, measure traffic and, where applicable, track affiliate referrals. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though parts of the site may work less well. If we use analytics or advertising cookies, we will ask for your consent where the law requires it.

4. Email newsletter

When you subscribe, we store your email address to send you updates. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email. We do not sell or rent your email address.

5. Affiliate links and third parties

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click one and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Partners such as booking, tour, transport or eSIM providers may use their own cookies and have their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

6. Sharing your information

We share data only with service providers that help us run the site (for example hosting, analytics and email delivery), when the law requires it, or with your consent. We do not sell your personal data.

7. Data retention and security

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online service can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Children

Our website is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows when it last changed.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@travelmovemorocco.com or use our contact page.
